CASE 101 — JUST GREP
---------------------
Status: TRAINING EXERCISE

A logistics company suspects an employee is diverting shipments.
We pulled these files from the suspect's workstation:

  logs/log0.txt      Server log (raw socket traffic)
  comm/emails.txt    E-mail archive
  comm/contacts.txt  Address book
  docs/manifest.csv  Shipping manifest

A colleague already tried: grep "socket 001" logs/log0.txt
She found nothing useful on that channel. Pick up where she left off.

We have reasons to believe that a keyword going through one of the
sockets can be used to identify one of the emails.

ANSWER FORMAT
  <name>-<city>  (e.g. Brian-Copenhagen)

VERIFY
  echo -n "NAME-CITY" | md5
  Correct hash: ff85c5d47ce56d3dca0c7edc478ab483
