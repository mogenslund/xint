# Mission 001: The Kestrel Corridor

It is 18 September 2031. Relations between the Republic of Norland and the
Federation of Vesper have deteriorated. Vesper describes its activity near the
border as Exercise Autumn Shield.

Provide an assessment of the most likely major Vesper action during the next
48 hours.

Determine:

1. The acting formation.
2. The action it is preparing to conduct.
3. Its primary objective.
4. The most likely six-hour start window.
5. The five strongest evidence items supporting the complete assessment.

Your evidence should cover more than one intelligence discipline. Avoid choosing
five records that merely repeat the same observation.

## Validation

Construct one canonical string according to `reference/assessment-format.md`,
then calculate its MD5 hash. A correct hash matches one file under `validation/`.

The exercise contains plausible alternative explanations, stale reports, and at
least one deceptive indicator. A location being important does not necessarily
make it the primary objective.
